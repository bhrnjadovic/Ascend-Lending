Refinancers

A 20% deposit avoids LMI entirely. First home buyers may be eligible for government schemes that allow purchase with a smaller deposit without LMI. Yes. Self-employed borrowers have access to both standard (full doc) and low doc loan options. Standard loans require two years of tax returns and financials. Low doc options use alternative documentation such as BAS statements, business bank statements, or an accountant's declaration — making them more accessible for business owners with variable or complex income. We'll identify which approach suits your situation best.

A fixed rate locks your interest rate for a set period (typically 1–5 years), giving repayment certainty. A variable rate moves with the market — it can drop if rates fall, but rises if they increase. Split loans combine both. The right choice depends on your cash flow requirements, appetite for rate risk, and whether you plan to make additional repayments (most fixed loans restrict this).

Pre-approval is a conditional commitment from a lender indicating how much they're willing to lend, based on your financial position at the time of assessment. It gives you confidence when making offers on property. Pre-approvals typically remain valid for 3–6 months, depending on the lender. We recommend getting pre-approval before beginning your property search.
